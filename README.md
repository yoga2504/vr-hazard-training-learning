
# VR Hazard Training: Learning Project

**Status:** In progress, started 30 september 2026
**Author:** Yogashree Srinivasan, Curtin University
**Purpose:** Preparing for the HIVE Summer Internship project *VR Training Tool for Risk Management and Situational Awareness in Healthcare Settings*

## Why this project
Community healthcare workers often work alone in unfamiliar homes, where
hazards are unpredictable. Classroom training can't fully recreate that
experience. VR lets learners practise recognising hazards and making
decisions in a safe setting, and research on immersive VR training in
healthcare reports increased self-efficacy among providers. I want to learn
how a real hazard becomes an interactive VR scenario, and later explore
whether realistic practice also builds competence, not only confidence.

## Goal
Build a small VR scene of a home-care hallway with a few hazards (for
example a trip hazard and a blocked exit). The user identifies the hazard
and chooses a response, then receives simple feedback.
